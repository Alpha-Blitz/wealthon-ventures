Process

Simple, structured, transparent.

01

You're introduced

Every capital partner joins through a referral or direct relationship. No cold calls, no ads.

02

We meet and discuss

We walk through how the firm operates, our trading approach, profit-sharing terms, and what the agreement covers. No pressure.

03

Agreements are signed

A Capital Partnership MoU is signed before any capital moves. Risk disclosure is explicit. No guaranteed returns. Ever. Documented.

04

Profits are shared quarterly

Each quarter, trading P&L is documented and profit distributions are made per the agreed ratio. Transparent. Written. Consistent.

Why trust us

Built on paper, not promises.

Every capital partner is protected by formal agreements before a single rupee moves.

Formal Agreement First

We sit down, discuss every term, and sign a Capital Partnership MoU before anything begins. You know exactly what you're entering.

The edge

Built on algorithms. Backed by data.

Our trading approach is systematic and research-driven. We apply quantitative models and algorithmic strategies across every market we operate in, removing emotion from execution.

Core methodology

Algorithmic Execution

Trades are executed through rule-based systems built on statistical models. Speed, precision and consistency — without manual bias interfering at the moment of execution.

Research

Quantitative Finance

We use mathematical frameworks to model market behaviour, price risk and identify statistical edges. Every strategy is back-tested before it touches live capital.

Risk

Systematic Risk Management

Position sizing, drawdown limits and volatility controls are built into the system. Risk parameters are defined before a trade opens, not after it moves against us.

Multi-market

Cross-Market Strategies

Our models run across Forex, Commodities and Crypto simultaneously. Diversification is built at the strategy level, not just the asset level.

Continuous

Strategy Iteration

Market conditions shift. Our models are regularly reviewed, stress-tested and updated to reflect current volatility regimes and structural market changes.

Infrastructure

Regulated Platforms

All algorithmic activity runs through internationally regulated, low-latency platforms built for professional trading operations.
